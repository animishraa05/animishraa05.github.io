# Feedback

I went through all 13 pages of the platform. Overall the structure is there — dashboard, courses, certifications, complaint process, etc. — but a few things stood out that might be worth cleaning up before we show this to anyone important.

---

## The Colours Don't Feel Like One Platform

If you click around the pages, you'll notice every section seems to use a different colour scheme. The front page is dark blue fading to teal, the Courses page is a different teal, the Sign In page is light blue fading to white. There are at least 5 different colour combinations happening.

For a government platform about workplace safety, it would help to pick one set of colours and stick with it everywhere. Makes it feel more put-together and trustworthy.

Also — the card shadows are a cold grey right now. On a cream/beige background, a warmer shadow colour would look more natural, like paper sitting on a desk.

---

## Text Size Keeps Changing

Headings, body text, and small labels all seem to use different sizes from page to page with no real system. Since a lot of users will be older government officials, keeping text sizes consistent and making sure body text is large enough to read comfortably would be good.

A couple specific things:

- Some paragraphs get really long across the screen — on bigger monitors they can go 100+ characters wide. For older readers, keeping text in a narrower column makes it much easier to follow.

- The orange "Sign In" button on the dark blue background is hard to read. The colours blend together a bit. A warmer red or deeper brown would stand out better.

- Some light text on dark backgrounds is also hard to make out.

---

## A Few Things About the Content

Since this is a platform about sexual harassment prevention, some imagery and wording might need a second look:

- The **About page** has an illustration of people holding a trophy. A trophy doesn't feel right for this subject — something about support or safety would be more appropriate.

- The footer mentions "Vikshit Guidelines" — just want to flag that it might be a typo for "Vishaka Guidelines," which is an important Supreme Court judgment related to workplace safety. Worth double-checking.

- **"Emergency Support"** is shown in bright red. Red can feel alarming, especially for someone already in a difficult situation. A calmer colour with clear text would still get the point across.

- The **SOS card on the Dashboard** is the brightest, most attention-grabbing thing on the page. For someone who's nervous about using a PoSH platform, having "SOS" be the first thing they see might make them more anxious, not less.

- There are a few legal terms like "Section 16 protections" and "Cognizable offence" that don't have explanations. Adding a simple note in plain language next to each would help people who aren't familiar with legal language.

- The Support page tabs switch between different styles — some are questions ("Is This Harassment?"), some are commands ("Know Your Rights"), some are just nouns ("IC Contact Directory"). Sticking to one style throughout would feel cleaner.

---

## Some People See Colours Differently

A few places use colour alone to show what something means — like orange for warning, green for good, blue for neutral. About 8% of men have some form of colour blindness, so adding a small icon or a word label alongside the colour would make sure nobody misses the information.

A couple buttons and menu items are also quite small — might be hard to tap for someone with less steady hands.

---

## The Dashboard and Other Pages Show the Same Numbers

The Dashboard, Risk Profile, and Intelligence pages all show mostly the same information — awareness score, engagement numbers, days until refresher. It's not obvious why someone would visit all three. 

Maybe each could have its own job:
- **Dashboard** — quick overview of where things stand today
- **Risk Profile** — how you compare to others and where you might be vulnerable
- **Intelligence** — whether things are getting better or worse over time

That way each page gives you a reason to visit it.

---

## A Few Small Things

- Card corners are sometimes sharp, sometimes rounded — picking one style everywhere would be a quick win
- Buttons don't always look the same across pages
- The Sign In page isn't very clear about whether you should pick your role first or just type your details in

---

## Things Worth Fixing First

If I had to pick what to focus on:

1. **Sort out the colours** — pick one palette, use it everywhere
2. **Fix text sizes** — make them consistent and large enough to read
3. **Fix the hard-to-read buttons** — especially the orange one on dark blue
4. **Add icons or labels alongside colours** — so colour-blind users aren't left out
5. **Change the trophy image and check "Vikshit"** — both are a bit off for a PoSH platform

The rest can be cleaned up as we go.
