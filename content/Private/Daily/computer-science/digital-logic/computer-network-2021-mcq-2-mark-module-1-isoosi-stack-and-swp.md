| Data Bits | d8 | d7 | d6 | d5 | d4 | d3 | d2 | d1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | 1 | 1 | 0 | x | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |

| Check Bits | c8 | c4 | c2 | c1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | y | 0 | 1 | 0 |

Which one of the following choices gives the correct values of x and y? [GATE||2021 ||SET1||MCQ || 2-mark ]
